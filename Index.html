About

About Ashwin Patel

Ashwin Patel leads SQUARE ARCHITECTS, providing professional guidance on civil engineering, architecture and construction planning for residential and commercial projects. From the first sketch of a floor plan to the practical realities of site conditions and structural requirements, Ashwin works directly with clients to bring clarity to the planning process before construction begins.

The practice focuses on home-building and renovation projects, offering planning support, architectural design, structural input and estimation guidance so that clients can make informed decisions about their property from the outset.
